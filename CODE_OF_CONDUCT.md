# Code of conduct

AuthorStyle aims to be a respectful, practical, and research-friendly community.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Discuss ideas and tradeoffs without personal attacks.
- Make room for beginners and non-native English speakers.
- Respect privacy, especially when discussing writing corpora.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained hostile comments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Dismissing privacy or safety concerns around user corpora.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.
- Building or promoting impersonation tools using someone else's writing without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub.
